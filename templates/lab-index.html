Cadence

Weekly routine.

One model a week moves from the backlog to a verdict. The full procedure lives on the methodology page.

  1. Pick the next queued model from the backlog, oldest entry first.
  2. Run the six battery tasks on the lab device with battery v1 prompts.
  3. Score each task PASS, PARTIAL, or FAIL against the shared rubric.
  4. Publish the per-model page with verdict, limits, and reproduction note.
  5. Link the model from the matrix and flip its backlog row to evaluated.
