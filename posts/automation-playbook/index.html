The automation playbook for solo builders

You don't need an AI platform. You need three habits and a folder named dist/ that always deploys.

1. Capture

One inbox. Everything goes there — voice notes, links, screenshots. Review weekly, archive ruthlessly.

2. Synthesize

Turn captures into one-pagers. If you can't explain it in 180 words, you don't understand it yet.

3. Publish

Static over dynamic. dist/ mirrored to public_html. Board approves, FTP ships, no CMS to patch.

# deploy (after Board approval)
lftp -e "mirror -R dist/ public_html/; quit" -u user,pass host
