Four labs shipped in one week, and buyers felt model fatigue

A month of coding-agent attacks, collected in one roundup

A security research roundup gathered the recent coding-agent findings into a single reading list, and the pattern is consistent: agents trust logs, issues and repository files that attackers can shape. Highlights include a firewall-log injection that reached cloud credentials, default agent action configurations that fell to a single unauthenticated issue, and skill files that scanners largely miss. The recommended posture is execution isolation, tight credential scope and disposable environments rather than reliance on detection.
