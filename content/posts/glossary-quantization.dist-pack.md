Full-precision weights are heavy. Quantization makes them light.

This glossary entry explains quantization in plain terms — storing a model's weights as compact lower-precision numbers, so it needs less memory and answers faster at a small accuracy cost. The standard move for running capable models on laptops and single-card servers.
