We started this site because the signal-to-noise ratio around AI collapsed. Every tool promises to replace you; few explain how work actually changes.

Our principles

  1. Essays over news. If it won't matter in 6 months, we don't publish it.
  2. Build in public, but edit in private. Drafts are messy, posts are tight.
  3. Fast and respectful. No popups, no trackers, 15KB CSS, dark by default.
Automation isn't about replacing humans — it's about removing the boring parts so humans can do the interesting ones.
