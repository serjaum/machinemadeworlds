Contrast, not brightness

We use --text: #ececf1 on --bg: #0b0b10 — off-white on deep navy-black. Pure white on pure black fatigues eyes.

Elevation with borders

Cards sit on --bg-raised with a subtle --border. In dark mode, shadows disappear; borders do the work.

One accent, one signal

--accent: #7c5cfb for brand, --accent-2: #00d9ff for links. Never both at once.
