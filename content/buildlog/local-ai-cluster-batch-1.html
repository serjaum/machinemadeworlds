Motivation

The local-AI topic needed a connected core: one guide that sizes quantized models per card, one that keeps embeddings resident beside the chat model, and one that joins both into a grounded retrieval loop. The three link to each other, to the glossary terms they extend, and to the topic hub, so readers can walk the whole arc without search. The live guides are quantized models explained, embeddings on consumer GPUs, and RAG on your own machine.
