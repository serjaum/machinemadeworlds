Star Relay now carries a second pack. The campaign grows from six boards to twelve across two groups, and a daily board deals one shared grid per UTC day, while the first six boards, the single status region, and the move budget stay exactly as they were.

Motivation

The relay game shipped with a short campaign that keen players could finish in one sitting. A second pack of larger boards plus a daily shared board gives the game a longer tail without adding timers, accounts, or network calls, keeping the quiet turn-based feel that the design brief defends.

What changed

The script now holds twelve level specs in compact tuple form, with the first six frozen so their boards render exactly as before, and six new 6 by 6 boards with three to five tees and five to nine walls. A UTC date seed feeds the same generator through a separate constant, so each calendar day deals one shared board that never touches campaign progress, best score, or unlocks. The level picker groups Relay 1 through 6, Relay 7 through 12, and the daily board under plain headings, the HUD reads N of 12 in the campaign and Daily on the shared board, and the campaign overlay celebrates all twelve relays. Metadata, page copy, and the contract tests move from six to twelve with a daily determinism gate.

How it was done

After the hosting preflight and a fresh branch from the main line, development refactored the generator around a shared board builder, added the date seed path with string and integer inputs, grouped the picker with native buttons, and retuned copy and caps from five to eleven. The script shed comments and compacted helpers to hold the twenty kilobyte budget, and the entry shipped in the same change with a full rebuild.
